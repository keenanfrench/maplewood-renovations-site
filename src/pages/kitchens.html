Questions

Kitchen FAQs

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Most of our kitchens take 6 to 10 weeks on site, after 3 to 5 weeks of design and joinery manufacture. Your fixed quote includes a written schedule.

Can we live at home during the renovation?

Usually, yes. We can set up a temporary kitchenette with a sink and benchtop, dust-seal the work zone and keep the rest of the house clean.

Do you handle permits and the trades?

Yes. We manage plumbing, electrical, gas, plastering, tiling, stone and any building permit needed for structural changes, all under one contract.

Can I supply my own appliances?

Absolutely. We design the joinery around your chosen appliances, or we can supply them at trade pricing.

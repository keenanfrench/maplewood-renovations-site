Behind the walls

Most bathroom problems start where you can’t see them.

A leaking shower is the most common building defect we’re called in to fix in other people’s work. So this is where we put the most effort.

  1. Sub-floor & framing check

    We open up, check for rot and moisture, and fix the structure before anything new goes in.

  2. Falls to the waste

    Screeded floors fall properly to every drain, so water goes where it should.

  3. Membrane & flood test

    A certified applicator lays the AS 3740 membrane, then we flood-test it for 24 hours.

  4. Photo record

    You get photos of every layer before it’s covered, so you know exactly what’s behind your tiles.

Questions

Bathroom FAQs

How long will we be without a bathroom?

A typical full bathroom renovation takes 3 to 4 weeks on site. If it is your only bathroom, we can arrange a temporary portable bathroom for the build.

What waterproofing do you use?

Every wet area is membraned to AS 3740 by a certified applicator, flood-tested for 24 hours and photographed layer by layer before tiling.

Can you move the toilet or shower?

In most homes, yes. Moving fixtures adds plumbing work and sometimes subfloor changes, which we’ll price clearly in your fixed quote.

Do you do ensuites and powder rooms too?

Yes. Ensuites, powder rooms and laundries are a big part of our work, often done alongside a main bathroom to save on time and cost.
